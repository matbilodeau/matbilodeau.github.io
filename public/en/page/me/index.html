Elevator pitch

I’ve always loved searching for the “whys and hows,” figuring out how a system can be bypassed, and, most importantly, how to improve it to make it resilient. My approach is focused on behavioral analysis and the human factor; I seek to understand the context and the intent behind an action to address problems at their root cause. Freedom of thought is one of my core values, and I strongly believe that knowledge should be freely shared. This is what drives my ongoing involvement in the InfoSec community.
